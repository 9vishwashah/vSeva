Step 404 / 08

Vihar entrysubmit karein.

Vihar ke baad Home par Add Vihar dabayein aur 4 chhote steps bharein.

1 · Date & type
Default aaj ki date; Morning ya Evening.

2 · Kiska Vihar & route
Sadhu / Sadhvi ki sankhya, From aur To. Distance apne aap aati hai.

3 · Sevaks
Saath chalne wale har Sevak ko add karein. Wheelchair / Car Seva ho to batayein.

4 · Submit
Samuday aur notes (optional), phir Submit Entry.
