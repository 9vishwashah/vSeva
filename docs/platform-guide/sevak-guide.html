After you sign in, vSeva asks you to complete your profile until every detail is filled. Tap Complete Now, or Later to be reminded on your next visit.

The six details that make 100%

Blood group · Date of birth · Family emergency contact name · Family emergency number · Occupation (if Other, add the details) · Address

Submit for approval10 / 17

Submit a Viharfor approval.

Every Vihar a Sevak records goes to the Captain first. It counts in your numbers once the Captain approves it.

1

Submit Entry

Fill the four steps and tap Submit Entry. One Sevak submits for everyone who walked.

2

Sent to your Captain

In My Vihars it shows Pending captain approval.

3

Captain approves

It now counts in your Stats, Sankalp and rank, for every Sevak on it.

4

If it is rejected

You get a notification. Check the details with your Captain.

My Vihars with a pending entry

Still pending? Remind your Captain. Please don't submit the same Vihar again.

Sankalp & Vihar Year12 / 17

Your year,your Sankalp.

A Vihar Year runs 14 October – 13 July. The months between are Chaturmas.

My Sankalp

Your Vihar target for the year. It starts at 5. Change it in Profile → My Sankalp → Edit. The ring on Home shows progress, like 1/5 · 20% complete.

Group Sankalp

Set by the Captain for the whole group. The Captain can turn off Allow Sevaks to edit their Sankalp to lock everyone's target.

Seamless Contact13 / 17

SeamlessContact.

More → Contacts

Emergency contacts

Added by the Captain, shown to every Sevak.

Sevak directory

Search a name, then tap Call or WhatsApp.

Notifications

The bell shows alerts. Tap one to open the right screen.

You are notified when

  • an upcoming Vihar is announced
  • your Captain records a Vihar with you in it
  • a submission is not approved, or one awaits the Captain
  • there is a new VChat message
  • you have had no Vihar for several days

Phone alerts: Profile → Push Notifications, and allow notifications for vSeva in your phone settings.

FAQ's16 / 17

FAQ's

I can't sign in.

Use the username from your Captain and your mobile number. Still stuck? Tap Forgot Password?

"Your session has ended."

Sign in again. Nothing you saved is lost.

My Vihar isn't in my stats.

It's probably pending. Check My Vihars, and remind your Captain.

A route is missing.

Ask your Captain to add it in Manage Routes.

My numbers changed.

Check the Vihar Year in the VY menu.

Save or Export does nothing.

Pick where to send the file in the share sheet that opens.

No notifications.

Profile → Push Notifications → Enable, and allow vSeva in phone settings.

Using an iPhone?

Open vseva.vjas.in in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen.
