Getting started05 / 21

Bring yourgroup on.

A group starts with its Captain. The vSeva team reviews every new group.

1

Apply

On the sign-in screen tap Create Captain Account and fill in the Captain, group and address details.

2

Wait for review

Once approved, the Captain signs in with their email as username and mobile number as password.

3

Check group details

Tap your photo → Profile & Settings → confirm names and city → Save Changes.

4

Set the Group Sankalp

In Profile & Settings, Edit Group Sankalp: Vihars the group aims for this year.

5

Add routes

More → Manage Routes: From, To, km and an optional Via.

6

Add Sevaks

More → Add Sevaks: name, mobile, gender. Share the login shown, in one tap on WhatsApp.

7

Add emergency contacts

More → Contacts. Every Sevak sees them.

Sankalp & Vihar Year15 / 21

Your year,your Sankalp.

A Vihar Year runs 14 October – 13 July. The months between are Chaturmas.

My Sankalp

Your Vihar target for the year. It starts at 5. Change it in Profile → My Sankalp → Edit. The ring on Home shows progress, like 1/5 · 20% complete.

Group Sankalp

Set by the Captain for the whole group. The Captain can turn off Allow Sevaks to edit their Sankalp to lock everyone's target.

Emergency contacts

Sevak directory

Notifications

The bell shows alerts. Tap one to open the right screen.

You are notified when

  • an upcoming Vihar is announced
  • your Captain records a Vihar with you in it
  • a submission is not approved, or one awaits the Captain
  • there is a new VChat message
  • you have had no Vihar for several days

Phone alerts: Profile → Push Notifications, and allow notifications for vSeva in your phone settings.

FAQ's20 / 21

FAQ's

I can't sign in.

Use the username from your Captain and your mobile number. Still stuck? Tap Forgot Password?

"Your session has ended."

Sign in again. Nothing you saved is lost.

My Vihar isn't in my stats.

It's probably pending. Check My Vihars, and remind your Captain.

A route is missing.

Ask your Captain to add it in Manage Routes.

My numbers changed.

Check the Vihar Year in the VY menu.

Save or Export does nothing.

In the app, pick where to send the file in the share sheet. On the web, check Downloads.

No notifications.

Profile → Push Notifications → Enable, and allow vSeva in phone settings.

Installing on iPhone?

Open vseva.vjas.in in Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen.
