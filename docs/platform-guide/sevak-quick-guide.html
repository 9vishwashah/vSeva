vSeva · Sevak Quick Guide
Step 404 / 08

Submit aVihar entry.

After a Vihar, tap Add Vihar on Home and fill four short steps.

1 · Date & type
Today by default; Morning or Evening.

2 · Vihar of & route
Sadhu / Sadhvi count, From and To. Distance fills itself.

3 · Sevaks
Add every Sevak who walked. Wheelchair / Car Seva if any.

4 · Submit
Samuday and notes if you like, then Submit Entry.

Step 405 / 08

After yousubmit.

One Vihar = one entry

Only one Sevak submits it, for everyone who walked. Others should not submit it again.

Until your Captain approves it, My Vihars shows Pending captain approval.

My Vihars with a pending entry

Wrong entry? Tell your Captain; they can edit it. Pending for long? Remind your Captain.
