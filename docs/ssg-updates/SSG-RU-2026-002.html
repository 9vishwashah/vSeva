This document follows the first Review and Update (SSG-RU-2026-001, 9 October 2026). It lists every new addition and update to the Shraman Seva Group app since then, based on the team's feedback between 9 and 15 October 2026, and closes with the points for our next discussion.

#AreaWhat is newStatus
A1Vihar entryMumukshu count"Vihar Of" now has Sadhu, Sadhvi and Mumukshu, each with its own count. An entry with only Mumukshu is accepted.Completed
A2Vihar entryMukhya Sadhu Bhagwant / Sadhviji BhagwantA new field below Samuday. Shown to the Captain in Pending Approvals.Completed
A3Vihar entryVihar Type: Internal / HighwayA toggle on the first step of the entry form, for both the Captain's entry and a Sevak's submission. Defaults to Internal.Completed
A4Upcoming ViharInternal / Highway when announcingThe same toggle in Announce Upcoming Vihar. The alert to Sevaks reads, for example, "Upcoming Highway Vihar on …", and the card and WhatsApp share show it too.Completed
A5DashboardInternal / Highway tileShows the Internal and Highway Vihar counts side by side for the selected Vihar Year. For a Captain it covers the whole group.Completed
A6PDF & Excel exportNew columns and summaryMumukshu, Mukhya Bhagwant and Vihar Type columns (each can be ticked or unticked). The TOTAL row adds up Mumukshu and the Internal / Highway split; the PDF summary gains Mumukshu and Internal / Highway boxes.Completed
A7DashboardShare badge with WhatsApp and InstagramThe share button now shows both logos and a rotating word (Share, Inspire, Impact, Motivate) with a light animation, to encourage sharing Seva achievements.Completed
A8StatisticsLast 7 days chart and monthly Consistency calendarA month-by-month calendar coloured by Vihars per day, with tap-to-see details. Leaderboards now sit above the charts.Completed
A9Sevak submission"Before you submit" guidanceA short note on the first step reminding the Sevak to select every Sevak who was on the Vihar.Completed

UUpdated in this release 12

#AreaWhat changedStatus
U1Vihar entrySave error resolvedAn entry could fail to save with a technical error when a Captain account was among the selected Sevaks. Entries now save normally in every case.Completed
U2Vihar entryCaptain accounts hidden from Sevak selectionOnly Sevaks appear when assigning Sevaks to a Vihar, for both the Captain's entry and a Sevak's submission.Completed
U3Sevak profile"External" renamed to "Highway"Vihar Type in the profile is now Internal, Highway or Both. Existing choices were carried over.Completed
U4Vihar entryMorning / Evening renamed "Vihar Session"So it is not confused with the new Vihar Type (Internal / Highway). In exports this column is now "Session".Completed
U5Whole appApp-styled selection screensThe phone's plain pickers are replaced with sheets in the app's colours: From / To locations, Vihar Type, Blood Group, Occupation and others. Long lists such as locations have a search box.Completed
U6Whole appClearer selected optionsThe selected option in toggles (Session, Vihar Type, Yes / No) is now solid saffron, so the choice is obvious at a glance.Completed
U7NavigationNew bottom navigation barA lighter, rounded bar with larger icons, a matching More menu, and the language menu moved into the header. Swipe left or right to move between pages.Completed
U8DashboardTighter, cleaner layoutEven spacing between stat tiles, "Your Stats", Share and Vihar Year on one line, and Active Sevaks as a single tap-to-open button.Completed
U9DashboardMy Sankalp card redesignedA compact white card with a saffron progress ring showing, for example, "2/10", a count-up animation and a soft pulse. Add Vihar stays the one orange action.Completed
U10View EntriesVihar card image alignmentThe saved and shared image of a Vihar card now matches the card on screen, with every value in its correct place.Completed
U11RegistrationCaptain registration formFields reordered with SSG examples, the pincode fills in the city, district and state, and District is recorded.Completed
U12Web appRepeated page reload resolvedssg.vjas.in could reload itself repeatedly on some phones. Fixed.Completed
